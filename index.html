About Me

Hello and welcome to my corner of the World Wide Web!

I am a Software Developer at Tich Apps Development. I mostly live in the JVM world, writing Java and Scala code that handles real data, streams and events.

I enjoy building event-driven systems with Kafka and SSE, keeping things simple and typed with ZIO and tapir, scratching pixels with libGDX and Swing, and occasionally hanging out in Go.
